# Loyalty Program BRD - Case Study

A practice Business Requirements Document (BRD) and process diagram for a mall customer loyalty program.

> **Note:** This is a practice case study. It is not a real project, and it was not validated with real stakeholders.

## Business Problem
A shopping mall has customer data but no structured way to reward and retain a segment of low-income, high-spending customers. This case study defines the requirements for a loyalty program for that segment.

## Dataset
[Mall Customer Segmentation](https://github.com/chiangyanika-gif/Mall-Customer-Segmentation) (public dataset, 200 customers).
- 52 customers fall in the low-income, high-spending-score segment.
- Segment averages: about 39.6k USD annual income and a spending score of about 65.
- The spending score is not actual money spent.
- The dataset does not show visit frequency, loyalty, retention, existing programs, or program costs.

## Process Diagram
The BPMN 2.0 swimlane diagram covers **enrollment, earning points, and redemption** (FR-01 to FR-04), with lanes for Customer, Cashier (store staff), and Loyalty System.

![BPMN diagram](Loyalty%20Program%20BPMN.png)

**Scope:** The diagram covers a customer who chooses to join the program. Customers who decline are out of scope.

## Assumptions
The following are assumed for this case study and were not confirmed with a real organization:
- Stakeholders and their interests
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Risks and mitigations
- Process steps in the diagram
- Success measure targets (to be set by the business)
